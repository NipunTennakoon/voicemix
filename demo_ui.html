How It Works

Source Voice

Upload an MP3 file with the voice you want to use. This provides the voice characteristics (pitch, tone, timbre) that will be applied.

Target Audio

Upload an MP3 file with the content you want to convert. The words/content will be preserved, but spoken in the source voice.

Voice Transfer

The system analyzes both files, identifies voice segments (filtering background sounds), extracts voice characteristics from the source, and applies them to the target audio. The result is the target content spoken in the source voice.

Example: If your source says "I love apple" in a female voice, and your target says "I live in Village" in a male voice, the output will be "I live in Village" spoken in the female voice.
